Job Apply Agent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Job Apply Agent is a personal tool used only by its owner, Srikanth Valluri, to prepare his own job applications. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive permission drive.file. With it, the app can see and edit only the files it created itself: a resume template (Google Doc), a job-tracking spreadsheet (Google Sheet), temporary copies of the template, and the resume PDFs it generates. It cannot see any other file in the user's Google Drive.

How the data is used

Those files are used solely to produce tailored resumes and to record job applications for the owner. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and deletion

Files stay in the owner's own Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the files can be deleted from Google Drive like any other file.
